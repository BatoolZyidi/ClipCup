Clip Cup — App Map

AI & OpenCV Video Pipeline · Architecture and Navigation Reference

App Overview

Clip Cup converts horizontal video (from local uploads or video links) into vertical shorts. It transcribes dialogue, identifies narrative highlights, uses OpenCV neural face detection and visual motion tracking to center the subject, and renders an audio-synced vertical clip with immediate cloud delivery.

Reference Code Route

1. Video Entry & Submission frontend/src/components/InputTabs.jsx

Receives user input (file upload or video URL), validates bounds, and initiates pipeline via POST /api/jobs.

2. Highlight Scoring & Dialogue Analysis backend/clipcup/pipeline/select_segment.py

Contains select_segment() and _highest_density_fallback(): evaluates speaking cadence, QA dynamics, and dialogue hooks across the entire video.

3. OpenCV 5 Tracking & Gaussian Stabilization backend/clipcup/pipeline/reframe.py

Contains reframe_video(): YuNet ONNX face detection, optical motion fallback, and bidirectional Gaussian kernel smoothing to eliminate all micro-jitter.

4. Result Presentation & History Navigation frontend/src/components/ResultPlayer.jsx & frontend/src/App.jsx

Displays vertical video preview, reframing metrics, full history restoration, and immediate "Back to Home" navigation.

To Change This, Start Here

  • Adjust short duration limit: Change MAX_SEGMENT_DURATION_MS in backend/clipcup/pipeline/select_segment.py.
  • Tune camera tracking sensitivity: Adjust window_size or deadzone_px in backend/clipcup/pipeline/reframe.py.
  • Modify frontend branding or buttons: Edit frontend/src/components/LandingPage.jsx and frontend/src/components/InputTabs.jsx.

A Practice to Reuse

Separation of Transient Processing from Persistent State: Heavy video processing runs in isolated per-job temporary directories that are guaranteed to be cleaned up in a finally block, while metadata and auth state persist in PostgreSQL. This prevents storage bloat, ensures user privacy, and keeps the server lean.
